# Procura client walkthrough

## Opening

Start at the homepage. Explain that Procura connects Saudi buyer teams with supplier businesses through company capability profiles, structured RFQs and comparable quotations. The directory is capability-based; it is not a product store.

## 1. Buyer request

1. Open **Create an RFQ** from the homepage or `/app/buyer/rfqs/new`.
2. Enter a title, category, delivery location and response deadline.
3. Add multiple material lines with technical specifications, units and quantities.
4. Add a local attachment if useful, then review the request.
5. Save a draft or publish the sample RFQ.

Explain that publishing runs a simulated rule-based match. It does not send an actual email or SMS.

## 2. Supplier matching

Open `/app/buyer/matches`. Show the criteria beside each supplier: relevant category and capability, service area, qualification records and verification status. Use the invitation action to show the prepared invitation state.

## 3. Supplier response

Open `/app/supplier/inbox` and review an opportunity. Accept or decline the invitation. For an accepted request, prepare an offer with brand/model, technical details, unit price, VAT, lead time and commercial remarks. Submit it to the sample workspace.

## 4. Buyer comparison and closure

Open `/app/buyer/compare` to compare technical fit, offered model, unit price, VAT-inclusive totals and lead time. Open a request detail page such as `/app/buyer/rfqs/RFQ-1026-001` to review the request timeline and close it after a buyer decision.

## 5. Marketplace operations

Open `/app/admin/verification` to review buyer and supplier business records. Then visit `/app/admin/matching`, `/app/admin/notifications`, `/app/admin/membership` and `/app/admin/reports` to demonstrate platform oversight and operational logs.

## Presentation notes

- All companies, registrations, RFQs, quotations and metrics in the workspaces are sample records.
- No email, SMS, billing or payment is performed by the prototype.
- Use the language control to switch the shell direction and review the Arabic interface treatment.
- Suggested short path: homepage → buyer RFQ → matching → supplier invitation → quotation → comparison → admin review.
